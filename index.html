Hi, I'm Danylo.

I'm an independent designer and developer. I started with websites for music projects and people I know. Now I build them for businesses around the world.

I care about the details you notice without noticing: how fast a page opens, how a button feels, how easy it is to call you. I work in English, Ukrainian and Russian.

Questions.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. I build a demo of your homepage at my own risk. If you don't like it, you simply say no.

What do I need to prepare?

Almost nothing. Your business name and a link to your Google Maps or Instagram page are enough to start. Photos and texts can come later.

How long does it take?

A demo takes a few days. The full site depends on the number of pages. We agree on the deadline before work starts.

Do you help with domain and hosting?

Yes. I can register a domain in your name, set up hosting and keep the site running after launch.

How does payment work?

Half when we agree on the project, half at launch. Payment by card transfer.

Can I edit the site myself later?

Yes. I'll show you how to change texts and photos, or I can do it for you.

Let's talk.

Tell me about your business or idea. I'll reply personally with a next step, or with a demo.
